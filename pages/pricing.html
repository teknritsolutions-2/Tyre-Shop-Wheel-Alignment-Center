Tyre pricing guidance

Why a tyre price needs the exact size.

Actual tyre prices vary by brand, pattern, size, vehicle, load/speed rating and current availability.

01 / SIZERead the sidewall

A format such as 205/55 R16 is a useful starting point. Load and speed ratings matter too.

02 / USEDescribe the driving

City, highway, mixed roads and regular load help narrow suitable patterns.

03 / STOCKCompare live options

Availability and brand promotions change, so a current size-based quote is more useful than a universal list.

Before approving work

Ask what is included.

Balancing weights, replacement valves, unusually sized wheels, damaged fasteners or extra corrective work may change the final price. A good workshop clarifies those additions before proceeding.
