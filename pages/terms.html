Practical boundaries for service information and this static website.

1. Service guidance

Website content is general information and does not replace inspecting the vehicle, tyre or wheel. A technician should confirm service suitability and repairability in person.

2. Illustrative pricing

Service amounts shown are starting-price examples for this website concept. Actual prices can vary with vehicle, wheel size, materials, required adjustment and condition. Tyre prices depend on exact size, brand, pattern and availability.

3. Appointment requests

A preferred time submitted through a future connected booking form would remain subject to workshop confirmation. The current static form does not send or reserve an appointment.

4. Third-party brands

Tyre manufacturer names are used only to describe a potential multi-brand range. TrueTrack does not claim official partner, authorised dealer or certified-centre status.

5. Driver responsibility

Drivers remain responsible for vehicle roadworthiness, following manufacturer guidance and acting promptly when a tyre or handling problem is suspected.
