Wheel services

Alignment and balancing.

Pulling on a level road, an off-centre steering wheel and uneven tyre wear are useful reasons to request an alignment check.

Balancing corrects uneven weight around a tyre-and-wheel assembly. Vibration at particular road speeds is a common clue.
