Alignment matters

A small angle can leave a visible wear pattern.

Alignment is worth checking when the car pulls, the steering sits off-centre or tread wear differs across the tyre.

  • Vehicle pulls to one side
  • Steering wheel sits off-centre
  • Uneven shoulder wear
  • Steering feels unsettled

Tyre condition guide

When should you replace a tyre?

Look for wear indicators, cracking, repeated pressure loss, sidewall damage and uneven tread. A visible sign is a reason to inspect—not a remote diagnosis.

  • Tread worn close to the indicators
  • Cracks, cuts or a sidewall bulge
  • Frequent or unexplained pressure loss
  • Uneven wear across the tread
  • Damage after a hard pothole impact
