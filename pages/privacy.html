Privacy policy.

A concise outline of how this front-end concept handles information.

On this pageScopeInformationPreferencesEmbedded mapContact

1. Scope

This policy describes the information practices represented by the TrueTrack Tyres website concept. A production business should review and adapt it before launch.

2. Appointment information

The appointment form asks for a name, phone number, email, vehicle details, requested service and preferred time. In this static version, the form validates information in the browser and does not transmit it to a server.

3. Local preferences

Theme and reading-direction choices are stored locally on the visitor's device so the selected display can be restored. They are not used for advertising or profiling.

4. Embedded map

The Contact page embeds a Google map. Google may receive technical information under its own policies when the map loads or a visitor opens directions.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@truetracktyres.example.
